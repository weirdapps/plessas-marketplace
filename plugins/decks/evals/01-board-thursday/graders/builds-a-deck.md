The response must produce, or be visibly producing, a **slide presentation** for
a board audience. It must not deliver a Word document, a memo, an email, or a
plain prose write-up as the deliverable.

Score 1 only if ALL of these hold:

- The deliverable is slides. A named .pptx file, or an explicit slide-by-slide
  structure, both count.
- There is a per-slide structure: each slide carries one message, rather than one
  undifferentiated wall of text.
- The 4.1m vs 5.3m gap, its two named drivers (issuing interchange, dormant
  cards), and the three approvals all appear.

Score 0 if the response only asks clarifying questions without committing to a
deck, or if the deliverable is a document, memo or email.
